QuantFut publishes statistical probabilities. It is not a bookmaker, it does not accept bets or money for betting, and it cannot guarantee you any result. If you decide to bet, treat it as entertainment and set limits.

Only for people aged 18 and over (or the minimum age required by the law of your country, if higher). If you do not meet it, do not use the service.

A high probability is not a certainty: the most likely forecasts also fail.

Simple rules for betting sensibly

  • Only bet money you can afford to lose. Never the money for rent, bills or savings, and never borrowed money.
  • Set a limit before you start, for both money and time, and stick to it whether you are winning or losing.
  • Do not chase losses. Trying to win back what you lost by betting more is the shortest road to a problem.
  • Do not bet in a hurry, when angry or under the influence of alcohol.
  • Take breaks and keep other hobbies. If betting is the only thing that interests you about sport, that is a warning sign.
  • Distrust any promise of a sure win, whoever makes it: there is no such thing.

Signs that gambling may be ceasing to be entertainment

  • You bet more money or more time than you intended.
  • You think about betting almost all day, or you become irritable when you cannot.
  • You lie to your family or friends about how much you bet.
  • You borrow money or sell things to keep betting.
  • You bet to escape problems or bad feelings.

If you recognise yourself in several of these, talk to someone you trust and ask for professional help. It is easier the sooner you do.
