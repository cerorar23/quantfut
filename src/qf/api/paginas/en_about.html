QuantFut publishes statistical probabilities for football, NBA and tennis, with the full record of its hits and misses. These are the legal entity and the rules behind it.

Who provides the service

QuantFut is the trade name of the service provided by Saptain LTD, a company incorporated in Seychelles, with its address at E Coast Rd, Victoria, Seychelles. For questions, complaints and requests about your account you can write to admin@pronosticosdeportivos.pro.

The terms of use and the processing of personal data are set out in the terms and the privacy policy; both are currently available only in Spanish.

What we do

  • We estimate the probabilities of sporting results with statistical models and compare them with market odds.
  • We record every forecast before the match using a chain of digital fingerprints, and we show afterwards whether it came true or not.
  • We explain how we do it, and its limits, in the methodology.
  • Part of the content is free and part requires a subscription.

What we do not do

  • We are not a bookmaker. We do not accept bets, we do not handle money for betting and we do not pay prizes.
  • We do not guarantee results. An estimated probability does not ensure that something will happen, and past results do not ensure future ones.
  • We do not give financial advice. The content is for information and entertainment.
  • The service is intended only for people aged 18 and over. See responsible gambling if gambling stops being entertainment.

How we work

We show misses as clearly as hits, and we do not edit a forecast once it has been sealed. If we make a mistake, we correct it and leave a record of the correction. You can let us know at admin@pronosticosdeportivos.pro.
