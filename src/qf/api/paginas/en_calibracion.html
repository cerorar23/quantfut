A probability is honest if it holds up: for matches we rate at 70%, the outcome should happen roughly seven times out of ten. Here it is measured using the data the site already publishes and compared against the betting market, whether the result favours us or not.

How to read the charts

Each dot groups matches given a similar probability by the model (horizontal axis) and shows how often they actually happened (vertical axis). The dashed line is perfect calibration. A larger dot means more matches; the vertical bar marks ±2 standard errors, the normal margin of chance; groups outside that margin are shown in amber. Below each chart is the same data in a table.

What it means and what it does not

Being well calibrated is not the same as making money: an honest probability can still be worse than the market, in which case there is no edge to use. That is why we do not promise profits. Forecasts are statistical estimates for information only; see the record of hits and misses (in Spanish), our methodology and, if gambling stops being entertainment, get help.
